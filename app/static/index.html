Explainable Machine Learning Architecture

SmartStock AI employs Scikit-learn Ridge Regression with day-of-week seasonality, rolling 3-day averages, and time-decay weighting for items with ≥ 7 days of consumption history. Items with fewer logs utilize a Weighted Exponential Moving Average (EMA, α=0.35) or rule-based category heuristics as an intelligent fallback.
